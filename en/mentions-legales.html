Nature of the service

TrustiScore gives apps, software and online services a grade from A to E assessing their digital sovereignty, according to a public grid based on five weighted criteria (see the method).

Assessments are based on public documentary sources, in particular terms of service, privacy policies, transparency reports, declared certifications and technical documentation published by the providers.

Each assessment is a documented appraisal, based on the information available on the date it was made and on the published methodology. It is neither a certification nor an exhaustive technical or legal audit of the apps assessed.

Grades are published for information purposes and are intended to inform users. They are not a personalised recommendation and do not replace each user's own judgement.

Any grade can be challenged, with public evidence in support, via the repository's issue tracker. Every substantiated challenge is reviewed and may lead to the reassessment of the criterion concerned and, where appropriate, an update of the overall grade.
